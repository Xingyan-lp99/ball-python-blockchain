## Tab 1

<ul><li><p>Gestures help people navigate, take action, or transform content</p></li><li><p>UI elements should respond to gestures in real time</p></li><li><p>Tap, scroll, and swipe are common gestures</p></li></ul>

<h2>Types of gestures</h2>

<h3>Tap</h3><p>People can navigate to destinations and interact with elements through touch.</p>

Mobile UI shows a tap on the 1st of 3 news cards. The card opens to show the full article.

<p>Tapping a card opens an article</p>

Mobile UI shows a double tap on a floral photo. The image expands to fill the screen.

<p>Double tapping on a photo opens it to full screen</p>
