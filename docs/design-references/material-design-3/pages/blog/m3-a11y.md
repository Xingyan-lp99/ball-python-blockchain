<h2>Let&rsquo;s get personal: Accessible design at I/O 2022</h2>
<p>This year at I/O, Material Design is putting a spotlight on accessibility. Our tech sessions, new guidelines, and developer learning pathways are designed to both introduce and expand upon Material's approach to accessible product design.</p>
<p>Last year at I/O we introduced <a href="https://material.io/blog/announcing-material-you">Material You</a>, an evolution of our design language that foregrounds personal preferences and adaptive capabilities. This year, we&rsquo;re excited to showcase how the dynamic color system is just the beginning of how Material helps you design and build for the needs of individuals.</p>
<p>In researching and building accessibility foundations for Material You, the design team resonated with wisdom found in disability studies: &ldquo;I try to position people with disabilities, in their own claimed bodies, as makers of meaning&mdash;rather than as surfaces reflecting the meaning of others.&rdquo; (Jay Timothy Dolmage, Disability Rhetoric)</p>
<h2>Launching at I/O 2022</h2>
<p>Take a look at our roundup of what launched this week to help you get started creating beautiful, personal, and accessible experiences.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>New guidelines</strong></p>
<p><a href="https://m3.material.io/foundations/accessible-design/overview" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Accessibility foundations</a> including new patterns and principles</p>
<p><a href="https://m3.material.io/components/switch/accessibility" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Redesigned switch component</a>, including color contrast and accessibility specs</p>
<p><a href="https://m3.material.io/foundations/accessible-design/design-to-implementation" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Introducing the design to implementation framework</a> that optimizes experiences for screen reader users and other modes of assistive tech navigation</p>
<p><strong>Tech sessions</strong></p>
<p><a href="https://goo.gle/io22-designing-accessibility" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Let&rsquo;s get personal: Designing accessibility for individuals</a></p>
<p><em>This session explores how Material Design&rsquo;s latest features enable designers and developers to build beautiful products for all. We&rsquo;ll share new research informing component updates and will walk through the ways that Material You generates color schemes with the necessary contrast ratios&ndash;by default!</em></p>
<p><a href="https://io.google/2022/program/33ca1afa-36ba-4f50-b016-0e5c3a3bf063/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google&rsquo;s Accessible Design Framework</a></p>
<p>Get started with best practices for design-to-eng handoffs with Google&rsquo;s framework for documenting structure, flow, and elements essential for defining accessible experiences.</p>
<p><strong>Codelabs</strong></p>

<p>Look out for more to come on Material.io this year as we continue to work on improving the experience of contrast and critical features for accessible components.</p>
