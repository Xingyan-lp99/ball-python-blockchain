<p>We are less than 3 weeks away from the <a href="https://goo.gle/MDA2021-Submission" target="_blank" rel="noopener">submission form</a> closing! Read on to learn about the 3 categories of Dark theme, large screen, and motion.&nbsp;<span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">The deadline to self-nominate is October 1, 2021; winners will be announced in December.</span></p>
<h2>Material Design Awards 2021</h2>
<p>The annual Material Design Awards help us celebrate the best in design from our talented Material Design community. This year, we&rsquo;re looking for products that use Material Design to deliver more dynamic, personal experiences through Motion, Large-screen design experiences, and expressive implementations of Dark Theme.&nbsp; <a href="https://goo.gle/MDA2021-Submission" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nominate your product</a> and review this year&rsquo;s <a href="https://goo.gle/MDA2021-Rules" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official rules</a>. Keep reading to learn more about the criteria for each category, and explore some of the latest resources for building with Material Design.</p>
<h2>Dark Theme</h2>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="text-align: center;">&lt;mio-image index=0&gt;&lt;/mio-image&gt;</span></p>
<p>Dark themes offer new ways of fulfilling user needs, whether it&rsquo;s by tailoring their experience for low-light environments, extending battery life to meet the demands of a busy day, or&mdash;in the words of Mike Scopino, design lead of <a href="https://material.io/blog/mda-2020-winners">2020 winner KAYAK</a>&mdash; reacting &ldquo;to [user] needs and preferences.&rdquo;</p>
<p>For 2021, we&rsquo;re looking for products that are both beautiful and legible, day and night, and that apply color and contrast thoughtfully to craft a harmonious and personal brand experience.</p>
<h4>Learn more</h4>
<p>Read Material&rsquo;s <a href="https://bit.ly/2M7hXv4">Dark Theme guidelines</a>.</p>
<p>Design a dark theme in this <a href="https://bit.ly/2XazIzW" target="_blank" rel="noopener">step-by-step tutorial</a>.</p>
<p>Create your own dark theme Material layouts with this <a href="https://bit.ly/2AiQPX8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">component library</a> for Figma.</p>
<p>Watch and learn how to <a href="https://bit.ly/36HAHL1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">design a dark theme with Material</a> and <a href="https://bit.ly/2yL76E8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">implement on Android</a>.</p>
<p>See how four Google products go &ldquo;<a href="https://bit.ly/3h5DUJb" target="_blank" rel="noopener">into the dark</a>&rdquo; with Material.</p>
<h2>Large Screen</h2>
<p style="text-align: center;">&lt;mio-image index=1&gt;&lt;/mio-image&gt;</p>
<p>How your design adapts is just as important as what&rsquo;s adapting: we&rsquo;re looking for products whose interfaces embody adaptability&mdash;from layouts to components&mdash; across device types and screen sizes, while staying true to their unique identity, personality, and functionality.</p>
<h4>Learn more</h4>
<p>Read an introduction to <a href="https://material.io/blog/material-design-for-large-screens">Material Design Guidance for Large Screens</a>.</p>
<p>Watch the <a href="https://events.google.com/io/session/b2f0dee6-b649-4e20-8ad6-69266e353f05?lng=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener">I/O on-demand session</a> on preparing your app for large screens.</p>
<p>Learn how to build an adaptive layout in this <a href="https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/adaptive-material-guidance?hl=en&amp;continue=https%3A%2F%2Fcodelabs.developers.google.com%2F#0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">step-by-step tutorial</a>.</p>
<h2>Motion</h2>
<p style="text-align: center;">&lt;mio-image index=2&gt;&lt;/mio-image&gt;</p>
<p>Used well, motion forges a natural link between users and their devices. Motion can communicate energy, and responds to touch with precision and personality. It can tell a story that guides a user forward by revealing information at just the right moment. This year, we&rsquo;re looking for products that come to life through elegant animations that make the complex feel simple and express style and brand effortlessly.</p>
<h4>Learn more</h4>
<p>Read the <a href="https://bit.ly/3dc7ISa">Material Motion guidelines</a>.</p>
<p>Start implementing <a href="https://bit.ly/2Xa86ef">Material&rsquo;s new motion system and transition patterns</a> on <a href="https://bit.ly/36DEBV8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Android</a> and <a href="https://bit.ly/36ERWwC" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Flutter</a>.</p>
<p>Watch and learn how to <a href="https://bit.ly/3ccxBQv" target="_blank" rel="noopener">implement motion with Material Design</a>.</p>
<p>Read <a href="https://bit.ly/2M8TzZT" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Making Motion Meaningful</a>.</p>
