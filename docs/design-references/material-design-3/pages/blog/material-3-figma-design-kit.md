<p><em>Tabula rasa</em> &ndash; a clean slate&hellip; Sounds great, right? Sometimes a fresh, empty Figma artboard feels full of opportunity, but sometimes it can feel too empty.</p>
<p>On a design team, creativity is communal and contextual. We experiment with forms, fonts, and colors; we ingest and layer onto ideas and sources around us.</p>
<p><strong>With the M3 design kit, UX building blocks are ready to use and customize in your Figma workflow. </strong>With premade UI elements and a library of connected components and styles, you can build from a foundation of the latest in Material 3.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.figma.com/community/file/1035203688168086460" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Check out the new design kit in Figma and make a copy to start using M3 in your files</a>.</p>
<h2>More than a sticker sheet</h2>
<p>&lt;mio-video index=0&gt;&lt;/mio-video&gt;</p>
<p>What&rsquo;s in the M3 design kit? Starting today, you get&hellip;</p>
<ul>
<li>28 UI components</li>
<li>169 Figma components in total</li>
<li>1,984 variants</li>
<li>429 styles</li>
</ul>
<p>And the design kit is still growing &ndash; it&rsquo;s up-to-date and kept in sync with the design guidelines on Material.io.</p>
<p>Beyond components, variants, and interaction states, the M3 design kit lets you <strong>inspect elements</strong> and <strong>apply semantic color roles</strong>. Color roles in the design kit are compatible with <a href="https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1034969338659738588/Material-Theme-Builder" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Material Theme Builder</a> so you can easily integrate a custom color scheme into your Figma workflow.</p>
<h2>How it works</h2>
<p>The new design kit <strong>uses Figma's most advanced features</strong>&nbsp;&ndash; we&rsquo;ve added metadata showing accessibility, color values, token names, robust variants, and interaction state details.</p>
<p>&lt;mio-image index=0&gt;&lt;/mio-image&gt;</p>
<h2>Get started</h2>
<p>&lt;mio-image index=1&gt;&lt;/mio-image&gt;</p>
<p>To get started, visit the Material Design community page: In the Material Design 3 design kit, select <strong>Get a copy</strong>.</p>
<p>You&rsquo;ll get a copy of the file, components, and styles to start using in Figma. Publish the file as a library to make the design kit available through the assets tab to you and your team.</p>
<p><strong>Let us know what you think!</strong></p>
<p>A lot of care, craft, and expertise went into the M3 design kit &ndash; we hope you try it out and send any feedback to the team. The Figma Community page for the design kit includes a changelog and more details on support; you can also get in touch with us via Twitter.</p>
<p>Last but not least&hellip;<strong>Thank you to everyone on the Material design system team</strong> for contributing and building out the design kit together.</p>
